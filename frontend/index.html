    Unlock Tessellate

    Choose a password, at least eight characters; a few unrelated words are best. The app asks for it when it opens, unless you turn that off in Settings. Ordinary notes are not encrypted and survive a reset, but anything you later mark private is locked with this password and cannot be recovered without it.

    Install the certificate

    Do this once per phone, so this address is trusted and never warns again.

    iPhone: press Install the certificate in Safari and choose Allow. Then, in the iPhone's own Settings app, General, VPN and Device Management, Tessellate, Install. Then General, About, Certificate Trust Settings, and switch it on.

    Android: open the downloaded file from the phone's own system settings app: Security, Encryption and credentials, Install a certificate, CA certificate.

    The first time this phone signs in, the computer the notebook is on asks whether to let it in: choose Allow there, and the phone opens by itself (it waits up to three minutes).

          Tessellate

          Today at a glance: your recent notes, reminders and widgets. Search finds notes, documents, files and app actions, and Ctrl+K opens the command palette for commands and places. First steps lists the three steps, ticked as you finish them; its menu can mark them all done or hide it, and Customise, Show first steps (or the palette's Show first steps) brings it back. Customise, then Edit layout, lets you add, move, resize and remove widgets. In the Focused view the greeting card shows what is due today, your meetings, the notes to file and the note you were last in.

          Recording

          0:00

          The recorder keeps recording while its dialog is closed, and this bar shows the clock. Open brings the recorder back, Pause holds the recording and Resume carries on, and Stop ends it: the audio is kept in Library, Recordings and transcribed on this computer when the Voice notes add-on is installed.

          Live captions

          Live captions write what the microphone hears as you go, on this computer: the audio goes to a small speech engine that runs here, never to the internet. The line at the bottom is still being written and settles after a pause; the three above it are done. Stop saves everything said as a note tagged captions, and Copy takes it without stopping. Translate, when the offline translator is installed (Settings, Packages), writes each finished line in Spanish under the captions; the note keeps what was said. With Keep the audio of live captions on (Settings, General, Microphone), Stop also keeps the audio in Library, Recordings, with these lines as its transcript. Start and stop them from the command palette, Live captions. They need Live captions, installed in one click from Settings, Packages (about 32 MB); the first caption starts it.

              ↑↓ to choose · Enter to run · Esc to close · ? for shortcuts

              Type to find a command, a setting or a note. A few rows are small tools that run right here.

              • Timer. Type timer 10 minutes (or 90s timer, countdown 2h) and press Enter. The time runs on the status bar; press that chip to stop it, and Undo brings it back.
              • Stopwatch. Start a stopwatch counts up on the status bar; press it to stop.
              • Count words. Words, characters and reading time of the selected text, or of the editor.
              • A question. A line that ends in a question mark, or starts like one (how do I, where is, can I), is offered to Atlas first, with the commands that matched underneath.

              Keys and controls

              A board is a note you can draw on. Drop notes in from the Library, sketch, write in shapes and connect things: a connector follows whatever it joins, even rotated, and everything on the board stays searchable.

              Paste or drop an image straight onto the board. Right-click anything (or hold it on a touch screen) for its menu, and Ctrl+K finds any board action by its name.

              The top bar, left to right: Boards (back to the board list), which board or map to show, find a card on this board, board overview, the Edit menu (undo, select, duplicate, delete), View (how the board looks and which panels are open), Board (export, clear, help), the sidebar: shapes, icons and frames, your notes, and the layers, and toggle full screen; on a phone, More board commands holds the rest. A selected topic: + (add a child topic), the bookmark (add a child that points at a note, document, file or link), its corner square (resize this topic; Shift scales the words), and the chevron is collapse this branch or expand this branch.
