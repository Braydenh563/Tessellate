# graph100-1011 (Brief 100; INBOX 802, 864, 865, and the coordinator's "it kinda just appears after the page loads")

Built:
- (a) appearing and updating: settled positions cached per notebook (`gcSavePositions`, `gcCachedPositions`, localStorage `graphPositions`, up to 5,000 notes, keyed by seed and forces); the canvas fades in over twice `--ui-slow` (`gcReveal`); an update reheats at 0.3 from where the notes are (`GC_UPDATE_ALPHA`); new notes start beside their placed neighbours (`gcSeedBesideNeighbours`); leaving notes fade as their own sprites with their links (`gcMarkLeaving`, `gcDrawLeaving`, `gcDrawLeavingLines`, cap 2,500); the first map waits on a soft glow (`gcArriving`, graph-lazy.css `#graph-box.graph-arriving::after`).
- (b) hover: fade and hover eases read `--ui-slow` (`gcTokens.fadeMs`, `hoverMs`); `gcStill` (system hint or the app's Reduce motion and Performance mode) cuts them.
- Per-frame cost: edge looks cached per render (`gcEdgeLook`), no style objects or keys built per frame, the bow point into a scratch object, node sprites cached on the node.
- (c) nodes: `gcPaintCore` (an 8% radial shade, a 1px rim 25% deeper); the bloom block is byte- and pixel-identical; the open card's note wears the focus ring (`openId`). Sizes by links were already on a relative scale (`graphSizeRadius`); left as they were.
- (d) INBOX 802: the fit no longer reserves room for the minimap or the zoom strip (`GC_FIT_OVERLAYS`); every camera move glides on the token (`graphGlideMs`, graph.js); the worker holds a dropped note until the layout rests (`releaseDropped`, graph-worker.js).
- Dashboard constellation: holds 220px while it loads, a sheen while empty, a first sky fades in (`@starting-style`, 02-chat-graph.css); reduced motion cuts.
