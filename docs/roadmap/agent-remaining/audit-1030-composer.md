# Deep audit 2026-10-30: the composer (the no-model answer writer)

Auditor: Sonnet agent, read-only on source. Head 881588c (plus the merged `claude/sharp-thompson-45hngp`). Scope: `src/tessellate/ai` composer*.py, reading.py, acts.py, act_registry.py, commands.py, task_acts.py, note_rank.py, intent.py, and Ask and Chat behaviour. Ids Z1 and up. Every provider ran on a fake transport (CLAUDE.md 4); no real model was used, so the model-on rows test the composer's seams (the labelled facts, the grounding check, the why lines), not a model's prose.

How the answers were produced: `POST /chat/stream` with `answer_from:"notes"`, `notes_only:true`, `use_tools:false` (Ask) and the Chat body, on a seeded 43 then 49 note notebook (title-less one-liners, title-only notes, near-duplicate topics: Lisbon trip and Lisbon packing list, Sourdough starter and Sourdough loaf, Running plan and Running log, Tokyo trip, Project Atlas and Meeting with Priya, a defining note per topic such as Sara, Tom, Uni, Books, Budget). 663 single questions in 11 sets, 14 multi-turn dialogues in both Ask and Chat, 65 questions with a note open, 35 re-verification probes, 34 act phrasings, all run twice (a second full rerun on a coverage-instrumented server gave the same answers, apart from the notebook growing by the notes the act probes created and ticked). Every answer was read sentence by sentence. Caveats that matter when reading the rows: 25 October 2026 is a Sunday and the seeded diary note says "Saturday 25 October", and the Sara note was created on 25 September, so year and weekday findings built on those two carry a seed caveat (marked "seed" below); the clock was 30 October 2026, 02:50.

Rows are grouped by the rule that produced them (the owner's "fix the class, not the case"). Every row lists two or three other phrasings that fail the same way, so the fix is tested on the class. Ids already open or fixed in `audit-1029-ranked.md`, `composer-ranker-1029.md`, `composer-subject-1029.md` and `audit-1030-extras.md` are not re-listed; where one of them still reproduces on this head it is in the re-verification table at the end, not as a new row.

## Method and root causes in one place

- Latency: one loop-invariant call in `compare_sides` (`composer.py:837`) and an uncached candidate scan (`spelling.py:341`). Fix both and a typo question costs about what a right-spelt one does.
- Most "wrong sentence" findings share two causes: a snippet is quoted with a verb of speech (Z2, Z13, Z20), and the question's own words are treated as note words (Z16 to Z18, Z41). Fix those two rules and about a third of the rows change.
- Tests to add with the fixes: one table of the 663 questions with the expected lead sentence for 60 of them (a "golden lead" test), one for typo latency, and one that asks each count five ways and expects one number (Z10).
