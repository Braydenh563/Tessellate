Scope: the no-model composer (`src/tessellate/ai/composer*.py`, `reading.py`, `acts.py`, `act_registry.py`, `commands.py`, `task_acts.py`). Method: a seeded notebook of 49 notes (title-less notes, near-duplicate topics, a defining note per topic, an Atlas persona collision), `/chat/stream` driven over HTTP in both clients, seven multi-turn dialogues, five questions through the real Chat tab in Playwright. The provider is a fake transport and no model ran, so every row is the no-model composer; model-on parity (CHAT_PLAN decision 75) is not verified. Every row is marked `run` (reproduced by driving) or `code` (read only).

Today is 2026-10-29. The notebook "now" was Thursday 29 October 2026 (verified: 25 October 2026 is a Sunday, 14 August 2026 a Friday).
