## Ranked build list (each with its measure)

1. Blinks by mood and fatigue on the desk, one table both sides read (`vitals.blink` in the catalogue): blinks a minute and
   mean blink length per mood from the seeded probe; sleepy at least 1.25x calm's rate and 1.6x its length, curious and startled at most 0.8x the rate.
2. Breathing by exertion and mood, both sides (`vitals.breath`): desk sit after a walk or hop breathes at least 1.3x the rate of a sit
   after a stand; in-app the breath's playback rate after a walk at least 1.3, back under 1.05 within 40 s, sleepy at most 0.85.
3. Time-of-day reactions on both (`night_falls`, `morning_hello`): catalogue built on both, neither count 86 to 84; seeded screenwatch test (once a day each).
4. Full-screen return and typing burst on both (`fullscreen_app`, `typing_burst`): neither count 84 to 82.
5. Vertical follow-through (landing and hop throw the tail and wisps down then up), both: tip y offset in the 0.3 s after a landing.
6. A settle (exhale and shoulders down) 1 to 3 s after every startle, both.
7. Fixational micro-saccades during a gaze hold in-app (one compositor nudge of the pupils every 0.6 to 1.8 s while attending); the desk an eye-only look picture.
8. The desk stand's 4 s loop: a slow lean drift on the pose grid so no two cycles are the same picture (distinct pictures in 2 min over 24).
9. Media playing: the in-app `play` event (captured on the document) and the desk audio-session read, both a bob.
10. The life_rng seed fix above (re-baseline the seeded overlay tests that read blinks).
