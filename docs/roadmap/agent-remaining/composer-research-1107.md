# Composer research 2026-11-07: answer quality against the best answer-over-notes tools

Method: read-only. Comparison is from product knowledge of Obsidian Copilot and Smart Connections, Notion Q&A, Mem, Reflect, Apple Notes summaries, Perplexity and NotebookLM, not from fresh runs of those products. "We do" is from the code (file:line) and from `audit-1031-composer.md` (Fable, driven on 54 and 207 notes). Nothing here was re-driven on this head, and no real model ran (every provider test is a fake transport), so each row needs one reproduction before it is built. Rows already fixed per HISTORY are left out; rows still open per the 10-31 audit say so.

Ranked by what a person reading the answer loses (S1 wrong or missing note, then shape, then wording).

| # | Gap | Best apps do | Tessellate does | Smallest fix |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The defining note does not always lead | Smart Connections and Copilot rank by note-level similarity, so the note about X beats a note that repeats X. Perplexity leads with the most authoritative source. | `composer_digest._defines` (4460) scores 2 only for a title that equals the subject exactly, 1.5 for an opening "X is", 1 for filed-under. | 2 | Title-less notes get five names | Apple Notes, Reflect and Mem all name a title-less note by its first line, always the same way, with the date as a muted second field. | `composer_frame._clipped` (342, 7 words, "the note that starts “…”"), `composer_read.UNTITLED_WORDS` (29, 6 words), a bare "Untitled" fallback in `composer_notes.py:95,188`, `composer_study.py:188`, `composer_write.py:467`, and "your note, 24 July" in `composer_answer.cite` (177). | 8 | Quotes are altered | NotebookLM quotes verbatim and highlights the passage. | A day is written into a quote and "today" is replaced inside quotation marks (audit C28); first person is shifted to second inside a quote (C21, answer.py:`_shifted`). | Inside “…” never shift or substitute; shift only outside a quote mark.
