# dgai-1110: the AI in the diagram builder (DOCUMENTS_PLAN, "Placed from INBOX, 2026-11-10", the owner's "have you integrated the ai into the diagram builder yet??")

## The operation format (judge rung, decided here; build rows cite it as "dgai F<n>")

- **F1 One list of operations on the D1 model**, the same from the rule and from the model: `{"ops": [...], "said": "<one line>", "by": "rule" | "model", "dropped": <n>}`. Five verbs, nothing else:
  - `{"op": "add", "text", "shape", "after", "label", "group"}`: a block (`shape` one of diagram-ops.js's eight, or `"group"` for a group); with `after`, joined from that block with `label` as the arrow's words, and put in its group unless `group` says otherwise.
  - `{"op": "rename", "target", "text"}`: a block's words; `{"op": "rename", "from", "to", "text"}`: an arrow's words.
  - `{"op": "join", "from", "to", "label"}`.
  - `{"op": "nest", "target", "group"}`: `group` "" lifts it to the top.
  - `{"op": "delete", "target"}` (a block, its joins with it; a group lifts what it holds) or `{"op": "delete", "from", "to"}` (an arrow).
- **F2 A block is named by its id or its words**: the id first, then the words folded (case, spaces and punctuation; "In stock?" is "in stock"), and only when one block answers; a later op may name a block an earlier op added. A name two blocks answer drops the op.
- **F3 Untrusted, validated twice, never run.** The model's reply is read with `json.loads` on the first `{...}` or `[...]` in it, never evaluated. `ai/diagram_ask.py`'s `checked(ops, model)` plays the ops on a copy of the model and keeps only those that apply: a known verb, known keys, strings cut (words 120, arrow words 40), a shape from the list, names that resolve, the 120-block limit, at most 12 ops (`MAX_OPS`), and `dropped` counts the rest. The browser then applies them only through `diagramOps` (the pane's one writer), which refuses again; text lands only through `textContent`.
- **F4 The model is prompted** with `ASK_PROMPT` (under 700 characters, `tests/test_diagram_ask.py` holds it there): the five verbs with their keys, "name blocks by their words", "JSON only". The user turn is the diagram as lines (`B: In stock? [diamond] in Warehouse`, `B -> C: yes`), at most 60 blocks and 4,000 characters, then the ask (300 characters). A reply with no usable op falls back to the rule, and `said` says so.
- **F5 With no model, by rule** (`ai/diagram_ask.py`, beside composer_mermaid.py and its label rules): one clause per sentence or "and then"; `add|insert X after Y[ L]` (Y the longest block name the words start with, the rest the arrow's words: "after In stock? No" is Y "In stock?", L "No"), `add X between Y and Z` (join Y to X to Z, the Y to Z arrow goes), `add X` alone, `add a group X`; `rename|call|change Y to X`; `join|connect|link Y to Z[ with L]`; `put|move|nest Y in|into Z` ("out of its group" lifts it); `delete|remove Y`, `delete the arrow from Y to Z`. A text ending in "?" or the word decision is a diamond; start or end words a stadium.
- **F6 Suggestions are the same ops.** The lint (`diagram-ai.js`, pure region `DIAGRAM-AI-BEGIN`) finds: a decision's arrow with no words (rename the arrow yes or no), a decision with one way out, the other branch leaving the canvas (add an "End" step on the missing word), a block with no way out that is not an end shape (add an "End" after it), a block joined to nothing (join it from the block before), two blocks with the same words (rename the second "<words> 2"), a group holding one block (delete the group, which lifts it). Each row's Apply is the same preview, Apply and version as an ask.
- **F7 Preview, Apply, Discard, version.** The ops run on a copy; the picture beside the Ask box is the union of both drawn by the frame with three `classDef`s (added green, removed red dashed, changed outlined), the same drawing the Versions compare uses. Apply puts the copy in place with one `commit` (one source set, one undo step), then on a Documents page saves and names the version (`POST /documents/{id}/revisions`, "AI: added Refund"): the document versions API, no second history store. In a dialog (a Live figure, a board) Apply is one undo step and no version, because the host's history is the fence's or the board's.
- **F8 Versions in the builder menu** (Documents page only): the named and ordinary versions (`GET /documents/{id}/revisions`), pick two to compare (the F7 union picture of the older against the newer, with counts), and Restore (`restoreDocVersionWithUndo`, the history dialog's own path).
