## Row 1: Settings close

Cause: closing Settings (backdrop, X, Escape) calls `routerSettle`, which steps the browser back; `popstate` walks to the entry under Settings and `openHistoryEntry` ran `switchTab` on a tab that never left the screen, so `renderDashboard({refresh:true})` redrew the Dashboard under the closing window.
Fix: `openHistoryEntry(entry, { from })` returns when the entry left is Settings and the entry reached is the tab already active (navigation.js). Test: tests/test_settings_close_no_redraw_1111.py (node, stubbed).
Measured at 4x, backdrop click, six closes (before / after): onpopstate script 61, 79, 75, 100, 394 ms / 0, 8, 17, 20, 47 ms; first frame after the click median about 190 ms / about 110 ms; sum of long frames in the 2 s after the click median about 1,300 ms / about 350 ms. The first close of a session loads the quickNote bundle in both.

## Row 2: release read and notesSpyPick

Release (avatars.js): the tab's control boxes (`nameMarkBuddyControlBoxes`, 121 boxes on Notes) were not kept at the release in any of three probe drops (`nmb.covers` null before pointer-up), so the landing read them there; a throw also dropped them on purpose (`if (thrown) nameMarkBuddyIndexReset()`). Now `nmb.warmCovers` reads them after the frame that drew the pick-up (page clean) while it is carried, and a throw keeps boxes read since the pick-up (`drag.pickedAt`); the existing scroll, resize and mutation observers still drop them.
Measured at 4x, 36-note seed, Atlas on Notes, `nameMarkBuddyControlBoxes` called with the cache empty: 14.1 to 15.6 ms at the release (page dirtied by the figure's last write) vs 0 ms kept; the same read on a clean page costs 8.7 to 14.5 ms, so the full cost moves to the hold. The owner's 49 to 119 ms was a larger notebook; this seed has 121 boxes. The restyle of the figure's own rig (551 and 770 elements) in the release stays: it is the figure's last write, not a control read. Whole-release timings could not be compared: the machine's load average was 16 to 20 (release 733 to 2,147 ms, both before and after).
Tests: tests/test_companion_release_warm_1111.py.

notesSpyPick (notes-rail-spy.js): the settle timer called it directly, so with the layout dirtied inside the settle window it was the first reader (3.3 to 8.1 ms, median 4.8, ten runs at 4x; 13 to 16 ms on the owner's note). It now runs a frame later (`afterFrames` then a timer), when the layout is drawn: 0.1 to 1.4 ms, median 0.6 (two spikes of 40 ms in twenty runs under load, as the base had 49 and 146). A rect cache would not remove the layout, only move it into the frame; the frame wait does. Test: tests/test_notes_spy_after_frame_1111.py.

## Row 3: note clamp settle

Cause: `noteClampFit` (note-cards.js) unclipped each card inside the read loop, so the next card's `clientHeight` was a forced layout (51 in the owner's trace).
Fix: collect the cards that fit, then unclip them after every read. Test: tests/test_note_clamp_fit_batched_1111.py (node, fake cards; fails on the old code).
Measured at 4x, 120-note and 150-note long-note seeds (`seedlong.py`), 18 clamped cards handed to the observer in one batch (LoAF `forcedStyleAndLayoutDuration` of the ResizeObserver script), five runs: before 62, 41, 41, 59, 52 ms with 55, 34, 33, 45, 44 ms forced; after 6, 15, 6 ms with 0 forced (two runs had no long frame at all).
Not changed: 1667 m's frames with no script (100 to 250 ms at 4x on Library and Notes) are native style and layout against the 3.5 MB sheet; no handler to fix.

## Row 4: board step in the perf probe

New sweep `scratchpad/ui-sweeps/boardlag.js` (the probe had no board step): seeds one "Large board" of 400 text cards (`BOARD_OBJECTS`, 25 columns, through `/whiteboard/boards` and `/whiteboard/objects`; reused if it exists), opens it, moves the mouse 150 times across the canvas at `CPU=4` (default) and prints each long-animation-frame script by invoker with count, worst duration and worst forced style and layout, plus `LAG_RAF.owners` (lag-watch wraps every rAF callback, so the table is what names the callback).

Fix: `wbOnFrame(fn, after)`; the Select hover passes `true`, so the hit test runs in a timer after the frame has drawn (the row 2 pattern) and finds a clean layout. Port-drag hints keep the in-frame gate. Hints still appear on hover (8 anchor dots on a hovered card before and after). Measured after, three runs: 12, 2 and 2 long rAF frames, forced 33, 5 and 0 ms. The scan stays linear in items (about 3 ms a call at 1x on 400); a spatial index would need the rendered sizes the render already caches (`wbMapNodeSizeCache`), whose invalidation text edits do not all reach, so it was left. Test: tests/test_wb_hover_after_frame_1111.py.

## Row 5: graph crossings

Method: the app's own 36-note, 64-link seed (`/graph`: 36 notes, three hubs of 24, 5 and 6 links), the real worker run in node (`vm`, as tests/test_graph_untangle_1400.py does), organic shape, tidy on, three world sizes (1440x900, 1800x1400, 390x760) times six gravity values = 18 layouts; `settleUntangled` to the end, then `crossingsOn` over every line, and over the hubs' lines. The base is deterministic here (54.3 and 43.3 on both of two runs).

graph-1030.md:35, hub crossings. BUILT: `untangleFanSteps` (graph-worker.js): two of a hub's neighbours, of any category, trade spots when the lines on both cross less, both stay inside their category's reach (`untangleInside`) and clear of the rest, run for each hub at the end of each round inside the pass budget. Crossings, 18 layouts: 54.3 to 53.9 overall, 43.3 to 41.9 on the hubs' lines; applied to a finished layout of the base (9 layouts, deterministic): 53.6 to 50.9 crossings (an average of 2.7 fewer; no layout got worse). A small gain: the 36-note seed's three hubs link 35 of 36 notes, so most of what is left is structural. Tried and dropped, with numbers: (a) spreading the neighbours at even angles in the order their other lines lead, and (b) the same order read from the spots they stand in, turned both ways: 0 of 17 hubs improved; (c) swaps without the inside test: 51.5 (hub lines 41.4) but it leaves notes outside their category's reach, which `test_a_settled_map_is_untangled_without_moving_a_category_or_a_pin` rightly refuses; (d) swaps run inside the per-note loop rather than at the end of the round: 56.5, worse. Test: tests/test_graph_hub_neighbours_1111.py (a hub with two neighbours whose far lines cross: 5 crossings to 0, nothing moves when one is pinned).

graph19-1109.md:5, incremental crossing count per turned category. TRIED, NOT KEPT. A category turns whole, so two of its own lines never change their crossing; `crossingsTurning(inner, across, outer)` counted only the pairs a turn can change and returned the same decisions (same gain and the same positions to three decimals on 36, 150 and 600 notes). Time of one `untangleGroupsSteps` call, ms (base / new, two runs each on a machine at load 8 to 10): 36 notes 16 / 18; 150 notes 121 / 165 (random links), 40 and 51 / 38 and 56 (90% of links inside a category); 600 notes 2,726 / 1,674 (random), 256 and 238 / 488 and 200 (90% inside). graph-1111.md:24, a balance pass inside a padded layer. ## Row 6: block scope for the lazy-bundle reads

lzc-1101.md:4. BUILT in tests/test_lazy_bundle_calls.py (a test file, no app change): `_js_tokens` (strings, templates with nested `${}`, regexes and comments are one token or none), `_binding_names` (plain names, `{}` and `[]` patterns with defaults and `...rest`, `a = 1, b = 2` chains), `_ShadowIndex` (what each block, function body, arrow, catch and for head declares, from the parameter list's own bracket, plus where literal text is) and `_top_level_data` (the widened scan, kept as a superset of the old column-zero regex because the old scan also counted an IIFE-wrapped file's top level).

Two coarse rules that hid the problem are gone as well: a file's own names were "bound anywhere in the file" (`_defined_any_form`), and a bundle-mate's names were "bound anywhere in it". Both are the file's globals now (`_globals_of`: top-level functions, consts, classes and window exports), so a local in a sibling block no longer covers a real read of another file's const. The boot files' names for `always` stay any-depth (unchanged).

Numbers: the widened scan with no scope gives 9 false reads (`fit` x5 in sketch-pad.js, `bookmarkRow` in connections.js, ...); with the scope, 0, and the lint still reports the same real gaps (the existing chain probe) and every shape the scope must not hide: a binding in a sibling block, an earlier function, a finished `for` or `catch`, a longer name, a string or template text, an object key. Eleven chained names that were invisible (`libraryBaseItems`, `ocrWorkspaceCurrent`, ...) are candidates now; none is read from another lazy file. Tests: `test_a_local_of_the_same_name_is_not_a_read_of_the_other_files_const` (14 local forms), `test_the_scope_does_not_hide_a_read_no_enclosing_block_binds` (9 real forms), `test_a_chained_or_destructured_top_level_const_is_a_const_to_read` (four widened forms, late is a gap and early is not), `test_the_binding_scan_reads_patterns`. The suite file takes about 60 s now because each probe copies the frontend once (the probes run many cases per copy).

## Row 7: model-facts-1027 rows 6, 14, 15

All three in src/tessellate/ai/fact_check.py (and chat_said.py, routes_chat.py for row 6), tests in tests/test_model_facts_left_1111.py (49 cases, fake transport). Every provider test runs against the fake transport; whether a real model keeps to these checks is **unverified**.

- Row 6 (line 6, "nothing matched" with a model and no tools). BUILT: `fact_check.says_nothing_found(prose, question)` reads the negative: at most three sentences and 260 characters, each sentence a negation or a question back, no list, no figure and no name (capitalised word) the question lacks, no turn to something else ("but your note on Tokyo says"). `chat_said._miss_events` (called from `routes_chat._no_notes_answer`) hands the plain miss to the model as its facts (`build_facts_messages`), holds the reply whole (`_held_reply`) and keeps it only if the check passes; a failing, claiming or silent model leaves the engine's line and a stats row naming no model (the badge reads `stats.model` first). Scope found by driving it: the miss reaches `_no_notes_events` with a model up only when retrieval returns no note at all (a notebook of documents only, or an empty one); with any note in the notebook retrieval always hands the model a nearest note and the grounding heads-up already checks its words. An empty notebook keeps the engine's "You have no notes yet" with no model asked, because that answer is true and a model adds nothing to it. Not changed: the unknown-action, personal and unplaced branches ahead of it, the web chip and the related items behind it.
- Row 14 (line 14). BUILT in part, the rest by design. A yearless date whose two readings are within 90 days of each other in distance from now ("20 April" in November: 160 days ahead or 205 back) is read both ways, one date at a time (`_dated_spans`), so a correct "205 days ago" is no longer refused; the readings are alternatives, so no span between them exists ("365 days" stays refused). "5 January" (55 or 310) keeps its one reading. "Only to the nearest unit" is the rounding rule for spans (half a unit either way, `_span_kept`), the way people say a span; pinned by `test_the_nearest_unit_rule_is_the_rounding_of_a_span`, not changed.
- Row 15 (line 15). BUILT: `_known` reads the hours the facts state in `5pm`, `5:30 pm`, `5.30pm`, `5 p.m.`, `17:00`, `17h00`, `17.00` (quarter hours only: "2.50" is an amount), `5 o'clock`, `at 5`, `half past 5`, `quarter to 6`, `noon` and `midnight`. A real bug went with it: "5.30pm" was read as the hour 30 (known 6, 18, 30), so "by 5" about a 5.30pm reminder was refused. Not changed, by design: "by 5" about a reminder the engine did not list stays refused (nothing in the facts states the hour, so it cannot be checked).

Real-model check (unverified, needs the local model): `bash scratchpad/llama-dev.sh serve`, then `PYTHONPATH=src .venv/bin/python -m pytest -m evals`, and by hand in Chat (Ask mode, no tools) with a notebook of documents only, ask "what do I know about zanzibar" ten times: count the log line "the model's words did not keep to the app's miss" (a refused reply) against the kept ones. A refused reply costs one model call and shows the engine's line; a kept one is the model's wording of the miss.

## Proposed CHANGELOG lines

- Closing Settings no longer redraws the page under it (the Dashboard re-render after a backdrop click is gone; the popstate handler fell from 60 to 390 ms to under 50 ms at 4x).
- A long note list settles its Show more buttons without forcing a layout per card (the ResizeObserver callback went from 41 to 62 ms with 33 to 55 ms forced to 6 to 15 ms with none, 18 cards at 4x).
- The companion's control boxes are read while it is carried, so letting go reads 14 to 16 ms less at 4x (a throw keeps them too).
- The notes rail's follow-the-reader pick waits for the frame that drew the change (3 to 8 ms of forced layout down to under 1.5 ms at 4x).
- The whiteboard's hover points (the dots a card shows under the Select tool) are found after the frame has drawn: on a 400-card board at 4x the long frames fell from about 100 to under 15 per 150 moves.
- The perf probe has a board step (`scratchpad/ui-sweeps/boardlag.js`, 400-card seed).
- The graph's untangling now also trades places between a hub's neighbours when that lowers the crossings (the 36-note seed: 54.3 to 53.9 crossings overall, 43.3 to 41.9 on the hubs' lines).
- The lazy-bundle lint reads block scope: a local, a parameter or a catch binding of the same name is no read of another file's const, so destructured and chained top-level bindings are now seen, and a name bound in a sibling block no longer hides a real read.
- With a model up and a notebook of documents only, "nothing matched" is the model's to say when its words are a plain negative (no find claimed, no figure or name the question lacks); anything else leaves the app's line.
- The fact check reads the hours facts state in more forms (`5.30pm`, `5 p.m.`, `17h00`, `5 o'clock`, `half past 5`, noon, midnight); `5.30pm` was read as the hour 30.
- A date with no year that could be either of two years (about half a year away) is checked both ways, so a right span is no longer refused.
