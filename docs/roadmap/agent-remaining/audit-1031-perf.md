# Deep audit 2026-10-31: perf (lag, long tasks, forced layout, per-frame loops, boot size, payloads, slow routes, complexity ratchet)

Fable suite, surface 13 of 13. Server on 8852 from head b3c65c4 (`serve.sh`,
fake model on 11600), the suite notebook (545 live notes, 19 boards, 17
documents, 386 links; the same data every other 1031 surface drove). Two
Playwright probes (`suite/hp1.js`: boot, nine tab switches, open note, palette,
idle rAF and idle requests at 4x CPU; `suite/hp2.js`: script census, typing,
scroll, listener growth over tab and note cycles, both at 1440x900 dark),
one curl pass over 28 routes (three runs each, best kept), the app's own lag
reporter log for the whole suite (`<data>/logs/lag.log`, 387 rows from every
surface's drive, build adf852440a to the current one), the static perf tests
and the complexity ratchet. Load average 5.8 to 6.8 on 4 cores throughout, so
counts (long tasks, layouts, nodes, listeners, bytes, requests) are trusted
over milliseconds; a millisecond here is an upper bound. Observed unless
marked code. Rows from the other 1031 surfaces that this pass re-measured are
named, not repeated.

## What is already good

- A tab at rest makes no requests for 30 s; listeners do not grow over library and notes cycles (7,029 five times).
- Server reads at 545 notes are all under 60 ms except three list routes, and the gzipped payloads are small (`/graph` 23 KB, `/entries?limit=50` 4 KB).
- Comments are stripped from the served page (776 KB from a 1.1 MB file); assets are hash-stamped and immutable for a year.
- The static budgets hold: boot JS 440,798 B of 448,400; boot CSS at its cap; `test_lag_layout_reads`, `test_perf_budget` and `test_perf_hot_paths` green.
- The lag reporter already names the forced-layout reader in most rows (PF1's evidence came from it), and the diag ring keeps slow requests server-side.

## Not verified

- Every millisecond above was taken at load average 6 on 4 cores; the counts are the evidence.
- 2,000 notes (only perf2, perf3 and GR1 hold those), a real model (HR3 holds the slow-model numbers), the phone width for boot (shell audit), long sessions for the PF6 growth.
- hp1's typing and list-scroll steps failed (the capture box was mid-autogrow and `#tab-main` does not exist); hp2's versions are the rows above.
